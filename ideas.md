# Caffetteria Mazzini — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Morning Counter
Very brief intro: A warm editorial café identity built around paper menus, espresso brown, and the quiet rhythm of a neighborhood morning. It feels human, tactile, and locally rooted.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Piazza Aperitivo
Very brief intro: A lively terracotta-and-citrus direction inspired by Italian evening light, striped awnings, and the social energy of aperitivo hour. It is expressive without becoming loud.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Mazzini Atelier
Very brief intro: A refined hospitality system pairing deep olive, cream, and brass with art-directed food photography. It positions the café as an intimate destination rather than a generic bar.
Probability: 0.02

## Selected approach: Piazza Aperitivo

### Design Movement
Contemporary Italian editorial hospitality: a meeting of 1970s café graphics, modern Swiss composition, and the sun-faded materiality of a historic Emilia-Romagna street.

### Core Principles
1. Make the café feel immediately walkable, local, and real.
2. Use asymmetry and editorial rhythm instead of generic centered cards.
3. Let terracotta, cream, and espresso brown create appetite and warmth.
4. Keep every important action one tap away: call, directions, menu, and Instagram.

### Color Philosophy
The signature color is burnt terracotta, chosen to echo brick façades, aperitivo bitters, and the warmth of late-day Castel San Giovanni. Cream keeps the interface tactile and legible; espresso brown grounds the page; a small lemon accent signals freshness and action.

### Layout Paradigm
A split editorial layout: oversized hero copy sits against a photo-led counter scene, while details break into offset panels and horizontal bands. The page should feel like a printed café menu unfolded across a table, not a stack of identical rounded cards.

### Signature Elements
- A small sun-disc mark with a coffee-bean cutout.
- Thin espresso-brown rules and ticket-like metadata labels.
- Terracotta action buttons paired with cream paper surfaces.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a physical menu: links reveal a little more, buttons press in gently, and gallery images shift with a subtle tactile lift. No interaction should obscure essential contact information.

### Animation
Use short 180–260ms ease-out transitions. Fade and translate content upward on first entry with a staggered rhythm. Use transform and opacity only. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Buttons compress slightly on press, and gallery images scale by no more than 1.03 on hover.

### Typography System
Use Fraunces for display headlines and editorial emphasis, paired with DM Sans for navigation, labels, and body copy. Headlines are large, slightly tight, and sentence case; metadata uses uppercase with generous tracking; body copy stays relaxed and readable.

### Brand Essence
A neighborhood café for Castel San Giovanni mornings, lunches, and aperitivo — made distinct by its intimate, social rhythm and a menu that moves from coffee to small-kitchen plates.
Personality: warm, lively, considered.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are sensory and local. CTAs are direct, useful, and friendly. Microcopy sounds like a host who knows the street, not a corporate chain.
Example lines: “A good reason to take the long way home.” / “Coffee first. Aperitivo later.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact circular sun-disc symbol with a central coffee-bean notch, paired with a custom all-caps wordmark that uses wide tracking and a slightly offset M. The symbol works independently as a favicon and menu stamp.

### Signature Brand Color
Burnt terracotta: #C95F3D.
